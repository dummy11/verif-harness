# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a welcoming, harassment-free community for participants of every
background and level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and technically specific.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Protect other people's confidential and personal information.
- Focus review on the contribution, not the contributor.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect a decision.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, publication of
private information, and sexualized or demeaning conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository
profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn participants, restrict
participation, or ban repeated or severe violations. Reports will be handled
as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest recused.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
